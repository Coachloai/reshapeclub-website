Privacy Policy

Last updated: 21 May 2026

ReShape ("we", "us", "our") is committed to protecting your privacy. This policy explains what personal data we collect, why we collect it, how we use it, and your rights under the UK GDPR and Data Protection Act 2018.

Who we are

ReShape is a nutrition and health coaching business operating in Ipswich and Colchester, England. For any privacy questions or requests, contact us at coach@reshape.fit.

What we collect

  • Contact details you provide — name, email address, phone number, and age.
  • Assessment responses — answers you give in our hormonal pattern assessment and related questionnaires.
  • Booking information — appointment date, time, and location when you book a consultation.
  • Usage data — pages visited, device and browser type, and interactions with our site, collected via cookies and similar technologies.

How we use it

  • To generate your personalised assessment results and send your report.
  • To contact you about your enquiry, booking, or coaching.
  • To send you follow-up emails and messages you have consented to receive (you can unsubscribe at any time).
  • To measure and improve our marketing, including advertising on Meta (Facebook and Instagram).

Advertising & analytics

We use the Meta Pixel and Conversions API to understand how our advertising performs and to show relevant ads. These tools may set cookies and share limited, hashed information with Meta. We also use Google Analytics to measure site usage. You can control cookies through your browser settings and our cookie banner.

Legal basis

We process your data on the basis of your consent (which you may withdraw at any time), to take steps at your request before entering a contract, and for our legitimate interests in operating and marketing our services.

Sharing

We never sell your data. We share it only with service providers who help us operate — for example email/SMS delivery, scheduling, and advertising platforms — and only to the extent necessary. These providers are bound to protect your data.

Retention

We keep your data only as long as needed for the purposes above, or as required by law. You can ask us to delete it at any time.

Your rights

Under UK GDPR you have the right to access, correct, delete, or restrict the processing of your data, to object to processing, and to data portability. To exercise any of these, email coach@reshape.fit. You also have the right to complain to the Information Commissioner's Office (ICO) at ico.org.uk.
