The 7 hormones that keep a woman young.

Aging in a woman's body is not a slow fade — it's hormonal. Each of these seven, kept in range, is what separates 35 from 55. The seminar walks through how to protect them.

Hormone 01
Estrogen
Bloated. → Balanced.
Thrown off by hormone-loaded meat and a sluggish gut. Reset it first — and the bloat, the mood swings, and the stuck weight start to lift.
Hormone 02
Insulin
Resistant. → Responsive.
When cells stop hearing insulin, every meal turns into stored fat. Cut the sugar and the signal comes back online.
Hormone 03
Leptin
Numb. → Satisfied.
The "I'm full" signal — drowned by fructose. Pull it back and your appetite finally tells you the truth.
Hormone 04
Cortisol
Strained. → Steady.
Caffeine spikes it; chronic stress keeps it high — and high cortisol parks fat on your belly. Reset the stress axis and the rest follows.
Hormone 05
Thyroid
Sluggish. → Lit.
Grains and gluten quietly inflame the thyroid — the metabolic thermostat. Lift the inflammation and energy returns.
Hormone 06
Growth Hormone
Soft. → Strong.
Dairy-driven inflammation suppresses recovery, repair, and lean tissue. Pull it for three days and the body rebuilds.
Hormone 07
Testosterone
Drained. → Driven.
Environmental toxins blunt testosterone in women too — flattening libido, drive, and muscle. A clean reset brings it back.
